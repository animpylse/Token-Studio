Solana Wallet Cleaning & Optimization

Flat Fee vs. Per-Asset Fee

Sol Incinerator charge a fee for every single NFT burned, which adds a fee transfer instruction for each asset. This bloats the transaction size and limits how many NFTs you can burn at once. Token Studio charges a flat fee per transaction, whether you burn 2 NFTs or 20, the fee is exactly the same. It's wildly more economical.

1232-Byte Maximum Payload Density

Because we don't bloat transactions with per-asset fee instructions, our custom packer can utilize the absolute maximum 1232-byte limit of the Solana network. This means you can pack and burn more assets in a single click than anywhere else.

Mixed Asset Processing

Burn NFTs, clear out scam tokens, and close empty token accounts simultaneously within the exact same transaction batch. No need to switch between different tools or tabs.

Wallet Health Reporter

A unique diagnostic tool that evaluates your wallet across multiple data points, including identifying empty token accounts. We tell you exactly what you can burn and close to improve your wallet health and get the most SOL back.

Bulk Burn NFTs & Scam Tokens

Select and burn hundreds of NFTs and tokens in a single click. Token Studio auto-sorts and tightly packs assets into full 1232-byte payload transactions.

Close Empty Token Accounts

Scan and automatically close unused accounts left behind by your onchain activity or improperly burned assets to recover locked SOL rent from back to your main balance..
